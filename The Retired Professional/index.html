The Framework

Five decisions that define a retirement.

Most retirements aren’t lost on a single mistake. They’re shaped — for better or worse — by a small number of choices. The book walks through each one.

01
When to claim Social Security
Age 62, full retirement age, or 70. The right answer depends on your household situation, your other income sources, and what survivor benefits will mean later.
Ch. 6–8Social Security Strategy
02
How to handle a lump sum or pension
Rollover, annuitize, or stay put? Each choice carries trade-offs around control, survivor protection, and how the money behaves over a 30-year retirement.
Ch. 9–13Lump-Sum Strategy
03
Where to draw income from, in what order
Sequencing withdrawals across taxable, tax-deferred, and Roth accounts can quietly extend the life of a portfolio — or shorten it.

How to plan for healthcare and longevity

What you leave behind

The Author
About Mark Buckley

A practitioner’s view, not an academic’s.

Mark Buckley is a retired Miami-Dade firefighter and paramedic and a Financial Professional with Prudential Advisors. He has spent his career on both sides of the work this book describes: first protecting the lives and homes of the people he served, and now helping families translate a working lifetime of saving into a retirement that can support them.

He has guided hundreds of retirees through the decisions that define retirement and is the author of Beyond The Firehouse: National Edition. The Retired Professional is the strategy guide he wished existed when his clients first walked through his door.
